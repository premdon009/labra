# Signing in

## What it's for

Signing in tells Labra who you are, so it can show only the screens and branches your
roles allow, and record your name against everything you do.

## Where to find it

Open the address your lab gave you (for example `https://lab.example`). If you aren't signed
in, Labra shows the **Sign in to Labra** page.

## Steps

1. Type your **Username**. Capital letters and spaces around it don't matter.
2. Type your **Password**.
3. Select **Sign in**. Labra opens the page you were trying to reach, or the home page.

To leave, select **Sign out** at the top right. Always sign out on a shared computer.

## Good to know

- **"Wrong username or password."** Labra doesn't say which of the two, so nobody can
  find out which usernames exist.
- **"Too many failed attempts."** After 5 wrong passwords from the same computer within
  15 minutes, that username is locked on that computer for 15 minutes. Wait, or ask an
  administrator to reset your password.
- **Signed out by itself.** After 30 minutes without using Labra, or 12 hours after signing
  in, you have to sign in again. Anything you hadn't saved is lost.
- **"This form has expired."** The page was open too long, or Labra was restarted. Reload
  the page and try again.
- **Passwords** need at least 10 characters, and common ones such as `password123` are
  refused. A short sentence is easy to remember and hard to guess.
- Some staff appear on records but never sign in, such as home-collection phlebotomists.
  Their **Signs in to Labra** box is unticked, so their username is refused here.
