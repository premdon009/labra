# Lab worklist

## What it's for

The worklist shows the tests your lab still has to do, the most urgent first. From here
you open a patient's tests and enter the results.

## Where to find it

**Menu ▸ Lab ▸ Worklist**, or press **Alt+W** on any screen. Technicians and signatories
see it.

## Steps

1. Check **Working at** shows your lab.
2. Open **Worklist**. Each line is one test: when it is due, the sample number, the
   patient's name, age and sex, the test, its department, and the branch it came from.
3. To see one bench only, pick a **Department** and press **Show**.
4. To see tests in one state, pick a **Status** and press **Show**. **To do** shows the
   tests that are on their way, in the lab, or waiting for verification.
5. Click the patient's name. Labra opens all of that visit's tests, at the test you chose.

## Good to know

- You see the tests that are done at the branch in **Working at**. A collection centre
  sees only the tests it keeps for itself.
- At the main lab, **Done at** also lets you look at a collection centre's list. You can
  read its results but not type them.
- **On its way** means the sample is collected but not yet scanned in at your lab. You
  can't enter its results until it is received.
- A test marked **(outside lab)** is being done by another lab.
- The list never shows the patient's phone number or address.
- The list shows 25 tests a page. Use **Next** and **Previous** at the bottom.
- A test leaves the **To do** list when it is verified.
