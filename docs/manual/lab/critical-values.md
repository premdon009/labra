# Critical values

## What it's for

A critical value is one so far outside the reference interval that the patient may be in
danger. Someone who can act on it, usually the referring doctor, has to be told at once.
This screen lists the critical values nobody has been told about yet, and keeps the log of
every call that was made.

## Where to find it

**Menu ▸ Lab ▸ Critical values**. The form to record a call is on the result screen,
in the row of the critical value.

## Steps

1. Enter the value as usual. Labra saves it and marks it **⚠ LL** or **⚠ HH**.
2. Phone the doctor, or tell them in person, and read out the value.
3. In the row of the value, fill in **Who was informed** and **How**, and **Their phone**
   if you have it.
4. Leave **When** empty if you have just told them. Otherwise enter the date and time.
5. Press **Record**. The row now reads **Informed**, with the name and the time.
6. Press **Submit for verification**.

## Good to know

- A critical value is saved as soon as you type it. It can't be submitted or verified
  until someone is on record as informed.
- **Nobody informed yet** at the top of the screen should be empty at the end of every
  shift. Click **Record who was informed** to go to the value.
- If you correct a critical value to another critical value, tell the doctor again and
  record it again. The log keeps both calls, each with the value that was read out.
- The time can't be in the future or before the visit.
- A log entry can't be edited or deleted.
- The phone number in the log belongs to the person you informed, not to the patient.
- Which values are critical is set with the reference interval. See
  [Reference ranges](../admin/reference-ranges.md).
- Technicians and signatories can open this screen. The front desk can't.
