# Handing over reports

## What it's for

Each released report has its own screen. From there you print the report or download it to
send, and record who it was handed over to. Labra keeps a log of every copy made and every
hand-over, with what was still due on the bill at the time.

## Where to find it

- **Menu ▸ Reports**, under **Released, not yet handed over**, press **Print or hand
  over** on the report's row.
- Or, on a visit's release screen, press **Open** beside a version under **Released
  versions**.

Front desk staff, branch managers and admins can record a hand-over. Technicians and
signatories can open the screen and make copies, but don't see the hand-over form.

## Steps

1. Check the heading: the report number, the version, the patient and the visit.
2. If the bill isn't fully paid, a red box shows **Due ₹… on the bill**. You can still
   print and hand over the report: tick the box that says you are going ahead although
   money is due.
3. To print or download, see [Print or send](print-vs-digital.md).
4. Under **Hand over**, fill in:
    - **Received by**: the patient, or who collected the report for them, such as "Patient's
      son".
    - **How**: at the counter, home delivery, by email or by WhatsApp.
    - **Handed over by**: you, or another member of staff at the branch, including staff
      who don't sign in to Labra, such as a home-collection phlebotomist.
    - **Note** (optional), up to 300 characters.
5. Press **Record the hand-over**. The report leaves **Released, not yet handed over**, and
   the log at the bottom shows the hand-over.

## The log

**Hand-overs and copies** lists, newest first, every copy printed or downloaded and every
hand-over, for every version of the report number:

- **When**, **Version** and **What**: PDF to print, PDF to send, or how it was handed over.
- **By**: who made the copy, or who handed it over.
- **Details**: who received it, or how many tests a copy of only some tests held.
- **Due then**: what was still due on the bill at that moment, if anything.

## Good to know

- **Email and WhatsApp are recorded only.** Labra doesn't send the report; send the PDF
  yourself, then record it here.
- Only the current version can be handed over. On a superseded version the screen links to
  the version in force, and you hand that one over instead.
- When a new version is released, such as after more tests are verified, it is listed again
  under **Released, not yet handed over**: the patient needs the new version too.
- Each hand-over also shows on the tests' timeline in the lab as **Report handed over**,
  after **Report released**.
- Who received the report is kept on the hand-over only; it is not written to the audit
  log.
