# Referrers

## What it's for

Referrers are the doctors, clinics, hospitals and companies that send patients to the lab.
A visit names its referrer, and the name is printed on the bill and the report.

## Where to find it

**Menu ▸ Referrers**. Front desk staff, branch managers and administrators see it. The list
is shared by every branch.

## Steps

To add a referrer:

1. Select **Add referrer**.
2. Choose the **Kind**: doctor, clinic, hospital, company or self.
3. Fill in the **Name** as it should print, such as `Dr Asha Rao`.
4. Add the **Qualification**, **Medical council registration** and **Phone** if you have
   them.
5. Select **Save**, or press **Alt+S**.

To change a referrer, select **Edit** on its row. To stop using one, untick **Still refers
patients**: old visits keep the name, but it is no longer offered for new visits.

To find a referrer in a long list, type part of the **Name** and select **Find**.

## Good to know

- A referrer can also be added from the [New visit](new-visit.md) screen, without leaving
  it.
- Use the kind **Self** for patients who come without a referral.
- Labra keeps no commission or payout details for referrers.
- If someone else saved the same referrer while you were editing, Labra refuses your save
  and asks you to reload.
- Every change is recorded in the audit log.
- Long lists show 25 rows at a time. Use **Previous** and **Next** under the list to move
  through them; a name you searched for stays in place.
