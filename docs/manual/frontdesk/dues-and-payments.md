# Dues and payments

## What it's for

See which bills still have money to collect, and take a payment when the patient pays the
rest.

## Where to find it

**Menu ▸ Dues**, or press **Alt+D** on any screen. Front desk staff, branch managers and
administrators see it.

## Steps

1. Open **Dues**. The oldest unpaid bill is at the top, with what was billed, what was
   paid and what is still due.
2. Select **Take payment** on the bill's row. You can also select **Take payment** on the
   visit.
3. Choose **Paid by**: cash, UPI or card.
4. Check the **Amount**. It starts at the full amount due; change it if the patient pays
   only a part.
5. For UPI, type the reference number from the patient's phone. For a card, type the last
   4 digits.
6. Select **Save and print receipt**, or press **Alt+S**. The receipt opens ready to
   print, and Labra returns to **Dues** afterwards.

## Good to know

- A payment can't be more than the amount due. Labra doesn't keep advances.
- A bill leaves the list as soon as it is paid in full.
- A payment can't be changed or removed after it is saved. If it was taken by mistake, a
  branch manager cancels the bill, which records the refund.
- Selecting **Save and print receipt** twice never takes the payment twice.
- Every payment gets its own receipt number and is recorded in the audit log.
- You see the dues of the branch shown under **Working at**.
- Long lists show 25 rows at a time. Use **Previous** and **Next** under the list.
