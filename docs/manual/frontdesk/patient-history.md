# Patient history

## What it's for

See everything a patient has had done: each visit with its tests and reports, and their
results from visit to visit in one table, so a doctor's question such as "how has the
cholesterol moved?" is answered at a glance.

## Where to find it

- **Menu ▸ Patients**, then **History** on the patient's row.
- **Patient history** on a report's own screen (see [Handing over
  reports](../reports/delivery.md)).

Front desk staff, branch managers, technicians, signatories and administrators see it.

## Steps

1. Open the patient's history.
2. **Results side by side** has a row for each result and a column for each visit, the
   newest on the left. A flagged value is shown in bold with its flag, such as **H**.
3. **Visits** lists the same visits with their branch, each test and where it has got to, and
   the report versions. Select a report to open it, print it or hand it over.
4. Use **Previous** and **Next** under the visits to move to older visits; the results table
   moves with them.

## Good to know

- Six visits show at a time, so the results table stays readable.
- Only verified or released results appear. A test still in the lab shows in the visits
  list with its status, but not in the results table.
- A result whose unit changed between visits gets a row of its own for each unit, so values
  in different units are never side by side.
- You see the visits of the branches you work at, and visits with a test done at your
  branch. A patient with no such visit is not found.
- A wide table scrolls sideways on a small screen.
