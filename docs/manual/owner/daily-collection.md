# Daily figures

## What it's for

Check a day's business at a glance at the end of the day: the money taken at each branch
by cash, UPI and card, the bills issued and cancelled, what is still owed, what the outside
labs cost against what the patients paid, and how many tests are still being worked on.

## Where to find it

**Menu ▸ Owner figures**. Administrators and branch managers see it, each for the
branches where they hold that role.

## Steps

1. Open **Owner figures**. It shows today.
2. To see another day, choose the **Date** and select **Show**.
3. If you look after more than one branch, choose one under **Branch**, or leave **All my
   branches** for all of them together.
4. Read the sections from the top:
   - **Collection**: the money taken at each branch's counter, by mode, less the refunds
     paid out there. **Net** is what should be in the drawer, the UPI account and the card
     machine together.
   - **Bills**: the bills dated that day, with their gross, discount and net, the bills
     cancelled that day, and how much was owed on all bills at the end of that day.
   - **Sent to outside labs**: the tests first sent out that day, what the outside labs
     charge for them, what the patients were charged, and the margin.
   - **Tests now**: the tests not yet verified, and how many are past their promised time.
5. Use the tabs at the top for **Dues**, **Pending tests** and **Outsourcing** (see [Dues,
   pending tests and outsourcing](dues-pending-outsourcing.md)).

## Good to know

- A day runs from midnight to midnight, Indian time.
- Money counts at the branch whose counter took it. A refund counts on the day it was paid
  back, so a bill paid yesterday and cancelled today lowers today's net.
- **Owed at the end of the day** is what was owed at that moment: a later payment or
  cancellation does not change an earlier day's figure.
- A test counts for the branch where the patient was registered, wherever it was done.
- A test sent out twice, for example after a sample was redrawn, counts once, on the day it
  was first sent. A test cancelled afterwards does not count.
- For a test in a package, the patient's charge is its share of the package price.
- **Tests now** is always the current count, whatever date you choose.
