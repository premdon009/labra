# Dues, pending tests and outsourcing

## What it's for

Follow up what the daily figures point to: which bills still have money to collect, which
tests are late, and how much each outside lab earns the lab over a period.

## Where to find it

**Menu ▸ Owner figures**, then the **Dues**, **Pending tests** or **Outsourcing** tab.
Administrators and branch managers see them, each for the branches where they hold that
role.

## Steps

1. **Dues** lists every bill with money still to collect at your branches, the oldest
   first, with its branch, patient, net, paid and due amounts. Select a bill number to open
   the visit and take the payment.
2. **Pending tests** lists the tests not yet verified, the most overdue first. An overdue
   test is shown in bold. **Where it is** says how far it has got, and **Done at** names the
   branch doing it. Select the visit number to open the visit.
3. **Outsourcing** shows, for each outside lab, the tests first sent there between the
   **From** and **To** dates, what the lab charges, what the patients were charged, and
   the margin. It starts on the first of this month up to today; change the dates and
   select **Show**.
4. If you look after more than one branch, choose one under **Branch** on **Pending tests**
   or **Outsourcing**.

## Good to know

- **Dues** shows the money owed now. The daily figures show what was owed at the end of a
  chosen day.
- A test counts for the branch where the patient was registered, wherever it was done.
- **Margin %** is the margin as a share of what the patients were charged.
- A test sent out more than once counts once, on the day it was first sent; a test
  cancelled afterwards does not count.
