# Code of conduct

Labra follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and patient, assume good intent, criticise ideas rather than people, and accept that maintainers have the final say on what gets merged. Harassment, personal attacks and sharing anyone's private information, patients' above all, are not tolerated.

## Reporting
Report conduct problems privately to the maintainers through the private channel in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md), marked "conduct". Reports are kept confidential. Maintainers may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone who breaks this code, following the enforcement guidelines of the Covenant.
